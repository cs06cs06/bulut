Halloween in the 1960s was a night that belonged to the kids. Bedsheet ghosts cut from the linen closet, plastic masks from the five-and-dime, pillowcases dragged home heavy with candy, and not a grown-up in sight.

In this countdown we remember 15 Halloween habits from across the United States and Canada that would never fly today, from Mischief Night soap and bobbing for apples in the washtub to the little orange UNICEF box and the Prairie chant of "Halloween apples." And number one came straight from the neighbor lady's kitchen.
