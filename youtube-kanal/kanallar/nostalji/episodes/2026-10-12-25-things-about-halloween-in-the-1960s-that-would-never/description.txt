Halloween in the 1960s was a night that belonged to children. Our costumes came from the hall closet or a thin cardboard box from the five-and-dime, our candy went into a pillowcase, and we walked every street in real darkness, usually with no parents in sight.

In this video, we remember 25 things about Halloween back then that would never happen today: popcorn balls from the neighbor lady, sweaty plastic masks, being invited inside for cider, the little orange UNICEF box, bobbing for apples, soaped windows on Mischief Night, Halloween apples on the Prairies and a parka over the costume.
