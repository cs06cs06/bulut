DESCRIPTION
Growing up in 1960s Canada meant snowsuits that made you waddle, mitts on a string, penny candy at the corner store, and Saturday nights in pyjamas begging to stay up for one more period of Hockey Night in Canada.

In this slow trip back, we count down 15 everyday memories: Dominion Day picnics, Canadian Tire money in the kitchen drawer, the polio vaccine on a sugar cube, the Eaton's wish book, centennial coins and that unforgettable bus trip to Expo 67. And number one? Almost every Canadian dad did it on freezing winter nights.

Which of these did your family do? Tell us where you grew up, and whether you called it a toque.
